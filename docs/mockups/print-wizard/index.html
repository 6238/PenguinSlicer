How to use this mockup The panel on the right is the wizard; go through it with Next. On its Parts step, click parts in the list or the picture on the left, as you would in Onshape. The two Part Studio tabs at the bottom (or Context) switch between the document's Part Studio in inches and its metric one without closing the panel, so a plate can mix inch and metric parts. Nothing is real: no slicing, no Onshape, no printer; a file you upload is not read, it becomes one of the mock parts, and Download saves nothing. Arm error makes a failure happen at the next chance; Printer sets what someone else is doing at the shop printer; Come back imitates a page refresh, closing and reopening the panel, or returning two hours later while work runs; 12 hours later moves the clock on with the panel open. Advanced mode shows a sample of Orca's settings. Drag the panel's left edge, or use Panel width, to widen it.
