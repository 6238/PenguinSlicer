How to use this mockup The panel on the right is the wizard; go through it with Next. On its Parts step, click parts in the list or the picture on the left, as you would in Onshape. Nothing is real: no slicing, no Onshape, no printer. Arm error makes a failure happen at the next chance; Printer sets what someone else is doing at the shop printer; Come back imitates a page refresh, closing and reopening the panel, or returning two hours later while work runs. Drag the panel's left edge, or use Panel width, to widen it.
