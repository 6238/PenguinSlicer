The Preview step's slicing and failures beside the mockup

Each pair is the mockup's whole panel (left) and the built page's (right) at the same width, in the same state. A failure's sentences differ: the mockup's are its own, the built page's the slice service's. The mockup's slicing card has Cancel slice, which the wizard leaves out on purpose. Made by make -C wizard look-preview-step.
