# Review of the architecture and the plan

The two documents are close but not ready for the owner: each reads well from first line to last, every anchor link resolves, and the architecture carries almost every decision word for word, but 13 findings concern decisions that were lost, moved or weakened and 9 links promise something their target does not hold. In [the plan](../../plans/penguin-slicer-plan.md), the most important fix is to restore to phases 2 and 3 what the approved design has them build (the slice container's half of restarts with its tests; two cookie keys, the rules for old and new versions, the command that reads the log bucket, the check after each deploy) together with the reason Railway and R2 were chosen. In [the architecture](../../architecture.md), the most important fix is to put back the one-day deletion rule at R2 and the size of a sliced file, which its own Security section points to under "The sliced file" and which are no longer there.

Reviewed Thu 2026-11-26, Pacific time, against the frozen
[hosted architecture design](../../specs/2026-11-25-hosted-architecture-design.md) ("the
spec") and [the map of its decisions](decision-map.md). In the bullets, "plan:N" is line N

Findings: Check A 20, Check B 13, Check C 8.
