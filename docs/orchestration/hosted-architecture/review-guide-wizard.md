# Review of the wizard's guide and of three contract files

The wizard's guide is close to ready but should not go to the owner yet: its body reads well and says plainly that nothing is built, yet the section the owner must act on, "Where the mockup and the design differ", is thirteen one-line recommendations with unexplained numbers and status words, and one of them (1.10) contradicts a sentence the guide states as fact forty lines earlier. The single most important thing to fix is item 1.10 in the details file, whose recommendation undoes the reasons given for items 1.1 and 1.2 and leaves the owner unable to decide it from the page. In the contract files, nothing of the approved design is lost, but the contract between the wizard and the front server contradicts the front server's guide in two places that a builder would trip on (which routes check the current group, and who keeps the sliced file's summary).

# The wizard's guide

## Check A: the guide reads well for its human reader

Read alone, first line to last, with only the plan known. Twelve findings, most serious first.

- guide:91-111, "Each has a recommended resolution: | Item | Recommended | Status |". This table is the owner's work list, and it is the form the owner's rule forbids: thirteen one-line answers with neither side stated. The numbers "1.1" to "2.5" are the comparison's section numbers and mean nothing on this page, and the two status words ("Awaiting the owner's choice", "Not yet confirmed") are never told apart. Fix: replace the sentence at guide:95 with "Each is set out in the details with both sides, what a user would see under each, and the recommendation with its reason, and is decided there, not from this table." Then add one sentence saying what separates the two statuses; neither file says it (details:405-406 only counts them), and the obvious reading, that a "choice" is a recommendation that is neither side's, fails for 1.9, whose recommendation is the mockup's. If nothing separates them, use one status. Make each Item cell a link to its entry (for example `[1.1 Sending the same plate again](README-details.md#11-sending-the-same-plate-again-after-a-long-print)`), and add after the first sentence "The numbers are those of the comparison the items come from."
- guide:65, "From phase 5, a page whose plate has a delivery job opens on the Send step." Stated as settled, while guide:109 recommends the opposite ("The Parts step, with the last-print note"). The reader is misled, or stops at line 109 and goes back. Fix: append "The mockup opens the Send step only while the print is running; which holds is item 1.10 below."
- guide:31-32, "They stay until the page, on opening, finds the plate's sliced file expired". "Expired" is used before the guide has said that a sliced file has a life at all; the two hours of a sliced file are never stated in the guide (line 38's two hours are the part store's, and line 99 gives only the recommended change). Fix: add before that sentence "A sliced file is kept for two hours after its slice ended (items 1.1 and 1.4 below may move either end)."
- guide:56-63, the table's rows "Unknown, submitted less than two hours ago" and "Unknown, submitted more than two hours ago". The guide dropped the spec's reasons, so the reader cannot tell why a slice job would be unknown or why the answer differs, and nothing says why the page kept the submission time (guide:31). Fix: restore the reasons ("Unknown, submitted less than two hours ago: the slice service restarted" and "Unknown, submitted more than two hours ago: its sliced file has expired"), and add under the table "The page tells the two unknown rows apart by the submission time it kept."
- guide:85-87, the bullet "Signing in." It sits under "Across a restart or a deploy", to which the cookie's thirty days, the current group and "sign in again" do not belong. The one rule of the browser's code about requests from other sites is absent, although guide:125 names it ("the header that proves a request came from the wizard's page") with nothing earlier to explain it. Fix: move the bullet into a section of its own, "Signing in and the current group", before "Across a restart or a deploy", and add "Every request that changes something carries a header that only the wizard's own page can set, because inside Onshape's panel the cookie alone does not prove a request came from the wizard." Leave under "Across a restart" only "A restart signs nobody out."
- guide:30, 101, 107, 109, 116, 127: "the current group", "the paired-printer record", "the last-print note", "a stale print", "stop following". None is in the plan's Terms table and none is defined in the guide; the first two are defined only in the details (details:64-67, 78-79). Fix: at guide:30 write "the current group (the one group, of those the user belongs to, whose configuration and printer the user is using now)"; at guide:101 "the paired-printer record (the one document for each group that names its daemon)"; at guide:109 "the note 'Your print finished at 11:25 am.'"; at guide:116 "a print whose daemon has gone silent"; at guide:127 "giving up a print whose daemon has gone silent ('stop following')".
- guide:86-87, "has 'sign in again' beside it, which reads the user's groups afresh for two calls to Onshape". The figure is given without what it bounds or why a user would use the action. Fix: "has 'sign in again' beside it: a user added to an Onshape company after signing in sees the new group only after using it. It costs two calls of Onshape's yearly allowance."
- guide:14-18, the sentence from "It talks only to the front server" to "runs the relay of printer delivery". One sentence with a link in parentheses, a colon and five clauses; it must be read twice, and the plan's Terms table already defines the front server. Fix: "It talks only to the **front server** ([its guide](../front/README.md)), the one process the internet reaches."
- guide:76-78, "through the front server, which sends a comment line at least every fifteen seconds". A figure of the front server's and the slice service's, with no statement of what it is for; a person reading about the wizard does not need it. Fix: cut "which sends a comment line at least every fifteen seconds"; the details keep it (details:215-218).
- guide:3-6, the opening paragraph. It says what is there and what is not, but not that thirteen items wait on the owner, which is the one thing the owner must do with this guide. Fix: add "Thirteen items await the owner's choice or confirmation; they are in 'Where the mockup and the design differ'."
- guide:4 and 93, "the approved design". Never linked, and the details use "the design" and "the architecture" for what may be two documents (details:5, 130). Fix: at guide:4 write "the approved design ([the architecture](../docs/architecture.md) holds it now)".
- guide:138, "The mockup is thrown away once this guide is complete." A bullet under "Still to be written" that is not a thing to write. Fix: move it out of the list as a closing sentence.

Length and order. 140 lines is right for this reader and the order of the body (what it is, what the page keeps, coming back, restarts) follows a plate. I would cut nothing else. The one move is the signing-in bullet above.

## The eleven items of "Where the mockup and the design differ"

Each item was checked against the owner's rule (decidable from the page alone: both sides, what a user would see under each, the recommendation with its reason) and against the comparison and the mockup's decisions. Every quotation is exact. Items 1.5, 1.6, 1.8 and 1.11 meet the rule. No item misquotes a side; three describe a side incompletely or label it wrongly (1.2, 1.7, 1.10). Ten findings, most serious first.

- **1.10, details:552-566. Not decidable, and its recommendation contradicts items 1.1 and 1.2.** Three things are missing. (a) The mockup's side leaves out that the Parts step it opens is empty: decision 48 says the notification "adds that the parts were not kept". With item 1.2's recommendation the browser's storage still holds the parts, so the entry must say which Parts step the user sees. (b) The page reloads itself at every deploy (details:274-276), and a reloaded page is a returning page. Under the recommendation, a user standing on the Send step after a print ended is moved to the Parts step by a deploy. Item 1.2's reason for its own recommendation is exactly that "a reload that dropped the layout would lose work at every deploy". (c) Item 1.1's reason is that "a user whose long print failed at the last layer can send it again at once"; under 1.10's recommendation that user loses the Send step on any refresh or deploy. Fix: state the three consequences under "What a user sees", and either change the recommendation to the design's (the Send step while the plate's sliced file lives, with the last-print note or the failure on it, as decision 40 shows it) or say in the reason why the user who wants to send again should go through the Parts step.
- **1.7, details:505-520. The recommendation is labelled "the design" but is neither side.** The design's configuration holds the filaments and the units only (spec lines 323-326); "the configuration names the printer's Orca profile" puts a printer into the configuration, which is nearer the mockup's side. Only this one answer is offered. The entry does not say who sets the profile (until the edit screens exist, the owner by hand through Cloudflare's console), what a user sees when it is absent or does not match the paired printer, or the alternatives: the daemon reports its printer's model at pairing into the paired-printer record, or every group uses the plate configuration's default set, the one H2S profile. Fix: retitle the recommendation "Neither as written", give the three options with what a user and a group setting up would see under each, and change its status to "awaiting the owner's choice".
- **1.1, details:408-425. The design's side is shown only as a defect.** "Under the design as written, Send is enabled ... for a file already deleted, and the send fails" describes a bug, not the design's choice. The real alternative is the design's two hours with an honest wizard: after two hours Send is disabled with the expired message and an offer to slice the plate again, which costs the user one slice (the parts are still in the browser's storage). Without it the owner is choosing between a recommendation and a straw man. Also "The mockup's promise then holds for every print" is too strong: it holds for two hours after the print ends, after which [screen 3.3](../../../wizard/README-details.md#screens-the-design-needs-that-the-mockup-does-not-show) applies. Fix: add the alternative with what the user sees, and write "holds for two hours after every print".
- **1.9, details:536-550. Its scope is not stated.** The mockup's decision 17 is about a part being added, and its error is "the part was not added". The entry's title and details:264-265 make it the rule for "every request". The entry does not say what a user sees after 20 seconds for a plate being submitted, a progress stream, a sliced file being fetched or a send. It also leaves out a third course the design makes possible: the part is already in the browser's storage (details:123), so the row could stay and go on trying, or offer "Try again", in place of dropping the part. Fix: say which requests the 20 seconds cover and what each shows, and add the third course.
- **1.4, details:459-470. M18 is an open question of the mockup, and part of it is lost.** M18 asks "Is dropping it right, and if so, how long should the server keep it?" (the mockup's decisions, line 233), and it is still in the list "Please react to these". The entry presents it as a decision and settles only when the two hours start; details:678 then counts M18 as covered ("M18 is item 1.4"). Whether to drop the slice at all, and for how long, would go unasked. Fix: add to the mockup's side "M18 is a question the owner has not answered: whether dropping is right, and how long to keep the slice", and to the recommendation "two hours stands, as the design has it, unless the owner answers M18 otherwise."
- **1.3, details:444-457. The reason overstates.** "another browser could not show or change the plate even if it found the slice job" is true of the Layout step only. The Preview step and Send work from the sliced file alone, and under the mockup the server also holds the plate configuration. Fix: "another browser could show the preview and send the plate, but could not show or change its layout".
- **1.2, details:429-432. The mockup's side is incomplete.** "the page reopens on the Preview step only for a running or finished slice" leaves out the Send step for a running delivery job, and decision 46, which says both and that the parts and layout are restored then, is not cited. Fix: cite "decisions 45, 46, 47 and 13" and write "reopens on the Preview step for a running or finished slice and on the Send step for a running print, with the parts and layout restored from the server".
- **2.4, details:622-630 (in the guide's table as awaiting the owner's choice). One answer only.** No second side and no account of what the user sees without it: after a restart of the front server an offline printer would show "Offline" with no duration, or a duration counted from the restart. Fix: add that alternative and what each shows.
- **1.8, details:522-534. One visible difference is unstated.** A notification goes when the user leaves the step (the mockup's decision 7), so the reason a part was refused is gone once the user moves on; a row would keep it. Fix: add that sentence to "What a user sees".
- **1.5, details:472-487. A fact that bears on the choice is unstated.** The four fixed colours were the owner's own instruction in version 8 ("version 8, the owner", the mockup's decisions, line 642). Fix: add "(the owner's instruction in version 8)" to the mockup's side.
